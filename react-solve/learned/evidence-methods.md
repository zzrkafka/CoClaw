# Evidence methods

This file stores transferable methods, supported by task evidence and approved by review, that primarily help a future Agent obtain and interpret discriminating evidence at low cost. A method should explain what it helps distinguish and when its signals may be misread rather than treating one observation as a conclusion.

Search it selectively according to the current judgment; when no relevant entry exists, let Codex choose its own inspection, profiling, or experiment. Keep new entries self-contained and, as the content requires, describe their conditions of use, interpretation boundaries, cost, and known limitations.
